Setup: 1 line vs everything else

With CronWatch, there's nothing to install, configure, or deploy. Just add one line to the end of your cron job.

✅ CronWatch
# Add to the end of your cron job:

  && curl -fsS https://cronwatch.io/ping/YOUR-TOKEN

Frequently asked questions

  • How is CronWatch different from healthchecks.io?
    Both use the same concept: your cron job pings a URL on success, and you get an alert if the ping doesn't arrive on schedule. The difference is price and simplicity. CronWatch is $9/month with a focus on a minimal, fast setup. healthchecks.io is open source with a more complex free tier (20 checks) and paid tiers starting at $20/month. If you're already on healthchecks.io and happy, no need to switch. If you're evaluating options, CronWatch is cheaper for most single-developer use cases.
  • How is CronWatch different from Dead Man's Snitch?
    Dead Man's Snitch is a solid product that's been around for years. Their entry pricing is $29/month — more than 3x CronWatch's $9/month. The core functionality is identical: add a curl line to your cron job, get alerted if the job misses. CronWatch targets developers who want simple, affordable monitoring without paying for features they don't use.
  • Does CronWatch work with GitHub Actions scheduled workflows?
    Yes. Add a final step to your workflow that runs curl -fsS https://cronwatch.io/ping/YOUR-TOKEN only on success. If GitHub Actions stops running your scheduled workflow (it does this silently after ~60 days of repo inactivity), CronWatch will alert you.
  • What happens if my cron job misses a beat?
    CronWatch checks for expected pings every few minutes. If your job's ping doesn't arrive within the configured grace period (default: 1 hour after the expected run time), you get an email alert. No SMS charges, no per-alert fees — just a plain email so you can go fix it.
